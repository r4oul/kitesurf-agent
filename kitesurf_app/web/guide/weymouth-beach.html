About this spot

A classic sandy beach town spot that comes alive in southerly winds. Quieter and less technical than nearby Portland Harbour, Weymouth Beach suits intermediate riders looking for a straightforward session. Out of season, it's one of the more relaxed spots on the Dorset coast.

Hazards

Busy beach in summer · Swimmers · Pier to the west
